\subsection*{S2. Right-to-disconnect coding}
Source: Eurofound (2023), \emph{Right to Disconnect: Implementation and Impact at Company Level}, Table~1 (pp.~8--9). EWCS 2024 fieldwork: February--November 2024; every provision below was in force before fieldwork. Primary coding = country covered if a statute was in force. Eligibility coding (sensitivity) counts as covered only employees inside the legal scope: teleworker/smart-worker laws $\rightarrow$ employees working from home at least ``sometimes'' (q29d $\le 3$); ICT-user laws $\rightarrow$ device use at least about a quarter of the time (q53a $\le 5$). The other 18 EU Member States are uncovered (not listed in the Eurofound review of all Member States). The review does not cover the eight non-EU countries (NO, CH, AL, BA, ME, MK, RS, XK); they are therefore \emph{unclassified} and excluded from the primary (EU-27) policy analysis; a sensitivity analysis over all 35 countries codes them uncovered. Self-employed workers are excluded from all analyses.

\subsection*{S3. Validation splits}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
\item Internal validation: stratified 5-fold cross-validation over employees (scikit-learn \texttt{StratifiedKFold(5, shuffle=True, random\_state=0)} applied to the analytic sample in file order); folds are generated deterministically by the released code (respondent-level fold IDs are not distributed because they are derived from licensed microdata).
\item Thresholds: inner \texttt{StratifiedKFold(3, shuffle=True, random\_state=1)} within each training fold.
\item Geographic validation: leave-one-country-out (35 folds, one per country); no assignment table is needed.
\item Region transfer: train on the 27 EU Member States, test on NO, CH, AL, BA, ME, MK, RS, XK.
\item Temporal validation: train on all EWCS 2024 employees, test on EWCS 2015 employees (S4).
\end{itemize}
\subsection*{S4. Complete specification and reproduction}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
\item Population: employees (q49 asked), poor-sleep outcome non-missing; $n=30{,}234$. Outcome = q86 $\in$ \{daily, several times a week\}.
\item Pipeline (per training fold): median imputation $\rightarrow$ standardisation $\rightarrow$ $\ell_2$ logistic regression ($C=1$, lbfgs, max\_iter 2000). The published equation (Table~I of the paper) is the final model refitted on all $n$ employees, with coefficients back-transformed to raw units: $\beta_j^{raw}=\beta_j^{std}/s_j$, $\beta_0^{raw}=\beta_0^{std}-\sum_j \beta_j^{std}m_j/s_j$ ($m_j, s_j$ = mean and SD after imputation). This is algebraically identical to the standardised model.
\item Raw coefficients: contact\_out\_wh -0.13803, freetime\_work -0.19056, norest\_b +0.17043, night +0.02882, longday +0.02183, loc\_home -0.00798, computer -0.00825, usual\_hours\_week -0.01147; intercept +0.4077.
\item Validation: stratified 5-fold CV (shuffle, seed 0); thresholds chosen in inner 3-fold CV inside each training fold (seed 1). The 0.150 threshold of the submitted version (chosen on pooled out-of-fold scores) is withdrawn. Decision-curve threshold probabilities (0.05--0.50) are a separate quantity, not an operating threshold.
\item Bootstraps: three separate percentile bootstraps of fixed out-of-fold predictions, 1{,}000 replicates each. B8 AUROC 95\% CI: worker-level 0.600--0.616; PSU-level (sampling point within country) 0.599--0.617; country-level 0.593--0.625.
\item \textbf{Harmonised seven-item model (2015 validation).} Same pipeline and learner, fitted on all 30{,}234 EWCS 2024 employees and applied unchanged to 35{,}308 EWCS 2015 employees (2024 medians used for imputation in both waves). Raw coefficients: freetime\_work -0.25502, norest\_b +0.20871, night +0.02967, longday +0.02406, loc\_home -0.00550, computer -0.01221, usual\_hours\_week -0.01143; intercept +0.1167. Imputation medians: free time 5, rest 0, night 0, long days 0, home 5, device 2, hours 40.
\item \textbf{Harmonisation 2024 $\rightarrow$ 2015.} free time q50 $\rightarrow$ Q46 (2015 asks ``since you started your main paid job''); rest $<$11\,h q41 $\rightarrow$ Q38; night q38 $\rightarrow$ Q37a; long days q40 $\rightarrow$ Q37d; home q29d $\rightarrow$ Q35e (2015 scale daily \ldots never, 2024 always \ldots never); device q53a $\rightarrow$ Q30i (same 7-point scale); hours q21 $\rightarrow$ Q24. Codes 7--9 (not applicable, don\textquotesingle t know, refusal) set to missing; monthly counts $>$31 and hours outside 1--100 set to missing. Outcome 2015: Q79a (difficulty falling asleep) or Q79b (waking repeatedly) daily or several times a week over the last 12 months; 2024: q86 sleeping difficulties daily or several times a week in the last month. Employees only (Q7 = 1).
\item Software: Python 3.9.13, scikit-learn 1.6.1, statsmodels 0.14.6, pandas 2.3.3, NumPy 1.26.4, SciPy 1.13.1; seed 0.
\end{itemize}
