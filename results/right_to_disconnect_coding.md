# Right-to-disconnect coding

Source: Eurofound (2023) Right to disconnect: implementation and impact at company level, Table 1 (pp. 8-9).
Fieldwork: EWCS 2024 main fieldwork Feb-Nov 2024; all provisions in force before fieldwork.
Other EU Member States: uncovered. Non-EU countries (NO, CH, AL, BA, ME, MK, RS, XK): unclassified (outside the EU-only legal source); excluded from the primary EU-27 analysis, coded uncovered only in the all-35 sensitivity analysis.
